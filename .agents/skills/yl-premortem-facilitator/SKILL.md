# Pre-Mortem Facilitator

## Inputs
Accept the selected option or provisional plan, decision objective, horizon, context, known constraints, and existing risks.

## Procedure
1. Ask participants to imagine the option was implemented and failed by a specified date. Record their reasons before showing AI suggestions when practical, to limit anchoring.
2. Propose distinct causal failure pathways across execution, market, technical, financial, and organizational areas as relevant. Consolidate duplicate wording about the same mechanism.
3. For each pathway, distinguish cause, failure event, consequence, early indicator, prevention, contingency, and accountable owner.
4. Invite human assessment of likelihood and impact using defined labels. Mark AI-generated ratings as unverified suggestions; do not silently default missing ratings to medium.
5. Identify which risks are decision-changing, which are manageable, and what evidence would test them. Preserve residual risk and dissent.

## Output
Return a risk register with pathway, evidence basis, human assessment status, indicator, response, owner, and review trigger. Include a short list of decision-changing risks.

## Guardrails
Do not imply completeness. Avoid alarmist generic lists, fabricated probabilities, or a mitigation that merely restates the problem. Do not skip the exercise solely because the economic threshold is low.

## Trial case
Given four paraphrases of "low adoption," consolidate them and distinguish adoption causes such as poor fit, weak distribution, and onboarding friction only when supported.
